# Update log

## 2026-11-03
* **Creation**: [A GPU GS on wgpu compute shaders](research/gpu-gs.md): the GPU renderer's design (compute, after paraLLEl-GS; one arithmetic shared with the software GS), milestones M0-M5; plan phase 9 updated. gs-replay counts transfers by direction: Sly never reads GS memory back.
* **Update**: The machine thread faster (VIF1 fed whole commands, UNPACK from words, XGKICK and the tag scanner cheaper): the prologue at 124% of NTSC, the machine alone at or above NTSC in every stretch; the dips left are the GS's ([graphics](research/sly1-graphics.md)).
* **Update**: The re-recorded prologue reproduces and runs at 120% of NTSC overall, 16 of 73 five-second stretches below (slowest 88%), some held by VU1 and the EE, some by the GS; the game clock matches the fields in play and runs 10% ahead in the opening cutscene ([graphics](research/sly1-graphics.md)).
* **Update**: Measured what unsafe would buy the GS (unchecked indexing: 2% on one thread, nothing on more) and how it scales on fewer threads ([graphics](research/sly1-graphics.md)).
* **Update**: GS: a pipeline specialised to the hot state, the decoded state shared across primitives, repeated footprints neither checked nor marked: 60 fields of play in 0.92 s on sixteen threads (was 1.10), the prologue's first 8,000 fields at 112% of console speed, local memory the same on twelve dumps ([graphics](research/sly1-graphics.md)).
* **Update**: GS: page tables give a pixel's word and shift directly (the play dump about 6% faster on one thread, local memory the same on twelve dumps); gs-replay --states counts pixels by pipeline state (90% of play is one shape of textured triangle) ([graphics](research/sly1-graphics.md)).
* **Update**: GS draws on up to 16 threads (was 8): the prologue's first 8,000 fields from 135.4 to 127.0 s, faster than real time ([graphics](research/sly1-graphics.md)).
* **Update**: GS: depth interpolated in 64 bits when it fits (the play dump 4.58 → 4.26 s on one thread, 1.26 → 1.20 s on eight, pixels the same); exact per-row stepping without divisions tried and dropped as slower ([graphics](research/sly1-graphics.md)).
* **Update**: Gameplay is now GS-bound: the drawer's pixel work is the critical path (60 fields of play take 1.30 s on 8 drawing threads, 1.18 on 16, against 1.0 s real time); replay reports host time and writes GS dumps, gs-replay reports the drawer's time ([graphics](research/sly1-graphics.md)).
* **Update**: VU microcode translated pair by pair into statements on locals, flags carried as four snapshots in micro::State, waits in place, pieces cut outside inner loops: 7.8 → 2.5 ns a pair, identical to the interpreter on every recorded program; the prologue at 76.5% of console speed ([VU translation](research/vu-translation.md)). The [proposal](research/vu-specialising-translator.md) is carried out.
* **Creation**: [A specialising VU1 translator](research/vu-specialising-translator.md): the design for the next VU1 speed-up, after in-place tuning stopped paying.
* **Update**: VU programs run in the VU float environment (new crate `ps2-fenv`), on host single-precision operations: translated microcode 11.5 → 7.8 ns a pair ([graphics](research/sly1-graphics.md)).
* **Update**: The VUs follow PCSX2's float model (exact result toward zero, no guard bits; zero and sign flags only): VU0 results agree with PCSX2's interpreter in 1,142 of 1,200 conformance lines, from 916 ([conformance](research/conformance.md)). Replay hashes and conformance/expected.txt re-recorded.
* **Update**: VU1 speed measured and compared with rac-rs; the generated game builds for the host CPU ([graphics](research/sly1-graphics.md)).
* **Update**: `[play.gamepad]` remaps gamepad buttons; the prologue plays through; gameplay runs at 56% of console speed headless, VU1 taking 84.5% of host time ([plan](plan.md)).
* **Update**: The pad reports a configurable DualShock 2 (modeConfig 2), so Sly 1 asks for analog mode and the left stick moves Sly after the binocucom ([boot](research/sly1-boot.md)); interface states renamed from UpdateUi.
* **Update**: `play` deinterlaces adaptively, with field parity from the GS: still 2D no longer jumps a line every field ([plan](plan.md)).
* **Update**: VIF0 starts with CYCLE 4, 4, as the BIOS leaves it, and a VIF reset keeps its registers, as in PCSX2: VIF0 had stalled at boot, so the lamps' lights were never found and the title's lamp lit nothing ([graphics](research/sly1-graphics.md)). Replay hashes re-recorded; `tools/pcsx2-frames.sh` takes a `state` press (a savestate).
* **Update**: VU flags now land four cycles after their instruction, as on the hardware: Sly 1's backface test in VU1 had culled the intro's and the title's sky, moon and Eiffel Tower ([graphics](research/sly1-graphics.md)). Replay hashes re-recorded.
